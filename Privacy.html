Rclone Bender — Privacy Policy Last updated: November 12, 2026 Rclone Bender is a personal application used by its owner to authorize Rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account for backup and restoration purposes. Data accessed The application requests Google Drive access to create, read, update, and delete files required for backup and restoration operations. Data usage Google Drive data is accessed only for the owner's backup management and restoration activities. Restic encrypts backup repository contents before they are uploaded. Data storage and sharing Rclone authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's systems. The application does not sell personal information or share Google Drive data with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data retention and deletion Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive account until deleted through the backup system or Google Drive. The owner can revoke application access through Google Account security settings. Contact Questions about this application or its data handling can be directed to the application developer using the contact email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
