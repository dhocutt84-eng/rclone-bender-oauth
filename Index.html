Rclone Bender Rclone Bender is a privately operated application used to authorize Rclone to access Google Drive for personal server backups. The application uses Rclone and Restic to transfer and maintain encrypted backups of a privately managed server. This application is not offered as a public service. It does not provide accounts or access to third-party users. For information about how Google Drive data is handled, see the "Privacy Policy" (privacy.html).
